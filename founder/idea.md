# hithozha

- What it is: A bilingual (Tamil/English) freelance marketplace app — clients post jobs or buy
  fixed-price service packages, freelancers deliver. A localized, Tamil Nadu-first Fiverr/Upwork
  hybrid, built on Expo/React Native + Supabase, with a web presence at hithozha.in.
- Who it is for: Tamil-speaking users in Tamil Nadu, India specifically — not generic
  India-wide. Two sides: clients (individuals and companies posting work) and freelancers
  (verified individuals delivering it), across 16 broad categories and 346 subcategories
  (writing, design, video, programming, marketing, business/finance, admin support,
  engineering, education, health, lifestyle/local services, gaming, web3, digital products).
- What it sells, at what price: Not a single product — a two-sided marketplace. Revenue is a
  commission on completed orders (default ~5%, reduced for freelancers on paid plans), plus
  freelancer and client subscription plans that raise open-job/package limits and lower
  commission, plus an internal admin-granted token system. Freelancers must pass identity
  verification before they can publish packages.
- Where and how: Mobile app (Expo/React Native) plus a web app at hithozha.in. Already built
  and live in GitHub; production deployment status is currently being sorted out (the Vercel
  project that should serve hithozha.in was found to still be linked to an older, unrelated
  repo, and the Supabase production database migration for a just-shipped 16/346-category
  taxonomy overhaul is pending).
- Budget and constraints: Already built, not pre-launch — this is a test of a business already
  in motion, not a new concept. No invented budget figures here; ongoing costs are Vercel/Supabase
  hosting plus Cashfree payment processing fees. Open questions for the board: real launch
  traction/user numbers are not yet established in this conversation.
