## What would flip a no

- If a few people I actually know -- in Tamil, not some English review on the website -- had used it and gotten their money back cleanly when something went wrong, and if I could pay via UPI on completion instead of upfront.
- If people in my own WhatsApp groups start saying they actually used it and got a good, fairly priced person -- or if my usual contact is suddenly unavailable and I need someone same-day -- I'd try it once to see.
- If a neighbour or someone in my building WhatsApp group had already booked someone real through it and said it went fine, I'd try booking too.
- If two or three people in my own apartment WhatsApp group had actually booked someone through it and said it worked out well, I would try it.
- If a neighbour in my WhatsApp group had already used it and said the freelancer showed up and did the job properly, or if the app clearly spelled out a no-show/bad-work refund policy -- then I'd try it once.
- A first-booking discount or referral code to actually push me to book, plus real customer ratings/reviews per freelancer (not just an identity check), and proof the Tamil experience is native, not translated.
- If two or three people in my own building's WhatsApp group actually used it and said it worked out well, I might try it once myself.
- If two or three people in my apartment WhatsApp group had actually booked someone through it and gotten what they paid for, I'd try it once.
- If two or three people in my own WhatsApp group had already used it and said the freelancer showed up on time and did good work -- then I'd try it, because that's basically the same trust I already use, just with an app attached.
- If someone I actually know -- a relative, a neighbour, someone in my building's WhatsApp group -- used hithozha for a repair or event job and told me it went well, I'd try it once myself.
- If someone in my own WhatsApp group had actually used it and vouched for a good experience, plus a clear written guarantee that a no-show or bad job gets refunded without a fight.
- If someone in my own WhatsApp group or a neighbour actually used a hithozha freelancer and vouched for them by name, I'd consider booking that same person through the app.
