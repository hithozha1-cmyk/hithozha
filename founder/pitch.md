hithozha, an app for booking local services in Tamil Nadu -- home repair, tutoring, beauty and
wellness, event help and more -- fully in Tamil or English, your choice.
Book Safe, Pay on Delivery: you don't pay upfront. Your payment is held and only released to the
freelancer once you confirm the job was actually done -- if they don't show up, or do a bad job,
you get a full refund from the held payment, no argument.
Every freelancer shows a real rating and completed-job count before you book, not just a
verification badge -- and you'll see how many bookings have actually happened near you, the real
number, even if that number is still small while we onboard the first batch of verified pros in
your area.
hithozha takes about a 5% booking fee from the freelancer, far below the roughly 25% other
services apps charge -- so the price you see is closer to what the freelancer actually earns.
Available now on Android and the web at hithozha.in.
