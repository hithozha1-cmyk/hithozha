hithozha, an app for booking local services in Tamil Nadu -- home repair, tutoring, beauty and
wellness, event help and more -- fully in Tamil or English, your choice.
Every freelancer who lists a fixed-price package has passed identity verification before you can
book them.
hithozha takes about a 5% booking fee from the freelancer, far below the roughly 25% other
services apps charge -- so the price you see is closer to what the freelancer actually earns.
Browse by category, see the price and the freelancer's profile up front, and book directly in
the app -- no calling five numbers to find out who's real.
Available now on Android and the web at hithozha.in.
